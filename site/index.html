CRANE

Five words. One guess each.

  • Each word comes with clues: guesses someone already made, with coloured letters.
  • Green is the right letter in the right place. Yellow is in the word, somewhere else. Grey isn't in it.
  • The clues leave only one common word. Find it in one try.

The clock starts when you press Play.

How to play

Every day there are five words: a 4-letter, three 5-letter and a 6 or 7-letter one.

Each comes with one or two clues: words someone already guessed, with coloured letters.

PILOT

P is in the word and in that spot. L is in the word but not there. I, O and T aren't in it at all.

The clues are chosen so that only one common word fits. You get one guess. Any real word that fits every clue counts.

Stuck? Need a hint? tells you one letter in its place. A word solved with a hint shows yellow in your results instead of green.

Answers are never plurals. A new set every day at midnight.
